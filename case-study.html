Case study · Armenia

MEDam.com

Armenia’s Official Medical Tourism Partner.

MEDam.com is where patients discover reviewed doctors and medical facilities in Armenia, hold a video consultation with their doctor before they travel and reserve securely through the platform, with prices up front. Bridged built the platform and licenses it to MEDam.com for use in Armenia. Treatments at launch: medical dentistry, plastic surgery, ophthalmology and hair restoration.

How it came together

  1. August 2024

    “Development of the sector is coordinated across the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Economy and the Office of the High Commissioner for Diaspora Affairs, which in August 2024 established a joint framework for the development of medical tourism in Armenia.”

  2. 2024

    Two directives are issued. Ministry of Health: “Directive to hospitals and doctors to expedite onboarding onto the MEDam.com platform.” Ministry of Economy: “Directive issued to hotels to accelerate onboarding onto the platform ahead of the tourism integration.”

  3. August 2025

    ARKA News Agency reports that Minister of Economy Gevorg Papoyan met Bridged to discuss promoting Armenia as a leading medical tourism destination and its impact on the economy.

  4. January 2026

    “Government Decision No. 30-L of 8 January 2026 approved the 2026–2030 Strategic Plan for Tourism Development of the Republic of Armenia, which identifies medical and wellness tourism as a developing area of the sector with strong growth prospects.”

  5. February 2026

    Armenpress reports the Tourism Committee’s announcement of the digital platform for medical tourism, alongside a second medical tourism conference held with the United Nations.

  6. May 2026

    MEDam.com operates under a Memorandum of Understanding with the Tourism Committee of the Ministry of Economy of the Republic of Armenia.

  7. August 2026

    “As of August 2026, 50+ doctors and 18 Medical Facilities have completed MEDam.com’s multi-stage review process ahead of public launch.”

  8. September 2026

    MEDam.com is live for patients. Discover. Consult. Reserve.

What Bridged licenses to MEDam.com

  • The patient platform behind MEDam.com.
    Discover. Consult. Reserve.
  • The provider review framework. On MEDam.com, every provider completes a multi-stage review before listing.
  • Onboarding and listing tools for doctors and medical facilities.

  • Concierge operations, MEDam.com offers support before, during and after your trip.
