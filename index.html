Mission

Our mission is to transform nations into world-leading medical tourism destinations.

By leveraging technology and industry best practices, we provide a blueprint that enables countries to offer a seamless and transparent one-stop solution for individuals seeking elective medical care.

Our blueprint has three parts

Government

Inside the national strategy

The program runs under memoranda of understanding with the ministries and tourism authorities.

Providers

A reviewed network

Every provider completes a multi-stage review before listing. Doctors and medical facilities in phase 1; hotels and tour operators in phase 2.

Patients

Three steps, one platform

  1. 1 DiscoverSearch reviewed doctors and medical facilities, with prices up front.
  2. 2 ConsultA video consultation with your doctor before you travel.
  3. 3 ReserveReserve your date securely through the platform.
